⚠ Understanding The Problem
Manual & Delayed Tracking: Scanning through hours of independent CCTV video archives causes catastrophic delays in apprehending suspect or stolen vehicles.
Single-Camera Silos: Existing cameras operate in isolation without spatial correlation, making city-wide vehicle trajectory reconstruction impossible.
Prohibitive Hardware Costs: Enterprise commercial ANPR systems demand expensive dedicated GPU servers (>₹2.5 Lakhs/camera), limiting scalability across tier-2/3 cities.
Ghost Plates & Clone Frauds: Criminals exploit duplicate or tampered registration plates; existing single-perimeter tools cannot detect duplicate vehicles in disparate zones.
Blindspot Inflexibility: Fixed camera poles cannot dynamically cover emergency checkpoints, VIP routes, or ad-hoc congestion points without new cabling.
✔ Our Solution
Multi-Camera Trajectory Graph: Automated chronological chaining of vehicle sightings across all roadside nodes with Haversine distance and speed analytics.
CPU-Optimized Edge Vision: High-performance OpenCV contour segmentation and normalized template matching running on standard laptops without GPU requirement.
Opt-In Mobile Camera Nodes: Turns any traffic officer's smartphone into a mobile ANPR node with live GPS tracking via secure URL (`/mobile-camera`).
Kinematic Anomaly Detection: Calculates velocity between camera hops ($v = \Delta d / \Delta t$); flags impossible speeds (>120 km/h) to instantly detect clone plates.
Live FIR Blacklist Matching: Instant cross-check against stolen vehicle records with automated priority escalation to nearest interceptor units.
MoRTH Standard Validation: Full Indian standard validation (`SS DD AA NNNN`) with optical character positional error correction.
Zero-State Integrity: Pure Live Mode with zero fake seed records; isolated on-demand demo dataset for stress simulation.
★ Our Unique Solutions
Dual-Node Infrastructure: First-ever unified architecture fusing permanent roadside CCTV streams with dynamic mobile phone nodes.
Honest Vision Architecture: Rejects fake plate cycling; blank, wall, or plate-free frames report zero detections honestly.
8-Second Deduplication: Automatically suppresses duplicate database inserts when a vehicle pauses in front of a camera.
Sub-Pixel Contour OCR: Robust CLAHE contrast enhancement for low-light, rainy, or dusty Indian road conditions.

⚙ Methodology
Optical Stream Ingestion: Captures 1080p RTSP H.264 streams from fixed CCTV nodes and real-time WebRTC frames from mobile smartphone nodes via `/mobile-camera`.
Vision Preprocessing: Implements CLAHE contrast equalization, bilateral denoising, and dynamic Otsu thresholding to isolate characters in high-contrast spaces.
Candidate ROI Segmentation: OpenCV contour hierarchy filtering isolates 4-corner bounding boxes conforming to Indian license plate aspect ratios ($1.8 \le ext{AR} \le 6.0$).
Modular OCR & Format Validation: Extracts normalized 20x30 alphanumeric glyph masks; validates against MoRTH standard regex (`^[A-Z]{2}[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{1,2}[0-9]{4}$`) with positional correction.
Kinematic Spatial Graph: Haversine formula calculates inter-camera velocity ($v = \Delta d / \Delta t$). Triggers `IMPOSSIBLE_SPEED` alert if velocity exceeds 120 km/h (identifying clone plates).
